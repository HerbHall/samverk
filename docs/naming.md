# Naming & Background

## The Name: Samverk

**Pronunciation:** SAM-verk (rhymes with "calm work")

**Language:** Icelandic / Old Norse

**Meaning:** Cooperative work
- *sam* = together, co-, shared
- *verk* = work, deed, task

**Why it fits:**
The name literally describes what the framework does — many agents working together toward a shared goal. Cooperative work is the architectural principle, not just a metaphor.

---

## Personal Connection

The project founder lived in Iceland. The Icelandic language has a deep connection to Old Norse — modern Icelanders can still read the original Viking-age sagas. Using an Icelandic word carries personal history while also grounding the project in something authentic rather than invented.

The Viking/Norse naming space is heavily claimed in tech (Sindri, Brokkr, Eitri — all taken by funded companies). "Samverk" is different: it's not a mythological figure or a proper noun, it's a common Icelandic word that happens to be a perfect conceptual fit.

---

## Naming Research Process

Over 30 candidates were researched across multiple categories before landing on Samverk:

**Norse mythology / blacksmith names (all taken in US tech):**
- Sindri — Sindri Labs (Denver, $7M funded, crypto/ZK infrastructure)
- Brokkr — Multiple companies (DeFi, industrial coatings)
- Eitri — Eitri Technology Solutions, Eitri Foundry, Eitri Apps

**Latin/Greek forge concepts (all heavily saturated):**
- Fabrica/Fabrik family — Dozens of conflicts including Fabrikam (Microsoft)
- Fabrex — Manufacturing software Spain, GigaIO's FabreX
- Fornax — Multiple Fornax Technologies companies
- Nexforge — NexForge Technology (defense/energy), Nexforge Technologies (hospitality AI)
- Opifex — Swiss software agency, Opifex Technology (AI/CV)

**Invented compounds (all taken):**
- Volantix — Travel companies
- Turmax — Automotive electronics
- Perfix — Perfix Inc exists
- Fundix — Investment/trading platforms

**Why Samverk survived:**
- Common Icelandic word (not a brand name in Iceland, just a word)
- No US software/tech company conflicts found
- The Faroese trade union using "Samverk" (samverk.fo) and Norwegian hotel co-op (samverk.com) are zero trademark risk — different country, different industry, different market entirely
- All-standard-Latin letters, no special characters needed

## US Trademark Considerations

Research scope was limited to US IP conflicts and market similarity sufficient to be actionable in a US court. Foreign companies using similar names in unrelated industries (trade unions, hotel cooperatives, glass workshops) are not a concern.

No funded, established US tech company was found using "Samverk" in a software/AI context.

*Note: This is research, not legal advice. A formal trademark search before commercial launch is recommended.*
