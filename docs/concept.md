# Concept & Problem Space

## The Core Problem

AI tools are everywhere, but most developers and small teams are underutilizing them — not because the tools aren't powerful, but because the *workflow* around them is broken.

### Pain Points Identified

**1. Context Collapse**
Every new AI session starts from zero. You re-explain your project, your conventions, your decisions, your constraints — every time. The AI has no memory of what you agreed on yesterday.

**2. Prompt Skill Gap**
The quality of AI output is directly proportional to the quality of your prompts. Most people get mediocre results, don't know why, and have no feedback loop to improve. The AI never tells you your prompt was bad.

**3. Tool Sprawl**
Teams end up with different people using different AI tools with zero consistency. No shared context, no shared quality bar, no shared workflow.

**4. Trust-But-Verify Gap**
AI output needs review, but there's no structured workflow for it. The developer is both producer and validator — context-switching constantly, often missing what they should be catching.

**5. Generic AI for Specific Jobs**
General-purpose AI wears all hats (researcher, developer, tech writer, QA, security reviewer) and produces mediocre results across all of them. Real teams have specialists.

**6. Organizational Knowledge**
Institutional knowledge — how we do things, why we made this decision, what we tried that didn't work — lives in no one's head and nowhere in the AI's context.

## The Samverk Vision

**Give solo developers and small teams the organizational capability of a full software company — powered by coordinated AI agents.**

The key insight: most AI framework projects are built for AI engineers. They require understanding of graph topology, agent orchestration patterns, token management, and prompt engineering. Samverk is built for *builders* — people who want to describe what they need and have a capable team handle it.

The mental model shift:
- ❌ "I need to engineer a prompt that makes the AI write good code"
- ✅ "I have a dev team — I describe the goal, they figure out how to execute it"

---

## Target Users

**Primary:** Solo developers and indie hackers who lack organizational bandwidth

**Secondary:** Small teams (2-5 people) who want consistent AI-assisted workflows without building infrastructure

**Not targeting (initially):** Enterprise, large teams, or users who need white-glove onboarding

---

## The Personal Connection

The name Samverk comes from Icelandic/Old Norse — "cooperative work." The founder lived in Iceland, and the name carries personal meaning while also being a perfect description of what the framework does: many agents working together toward a shared goal.

---

## What This Is Not

- Not a general-purpose chatbot wrapper
- Not another LangChain clone
- Not an enterprise orchestration platform
- Not trying to replace the underlying AI providers (Claude, GPT-4, etc.)

Samverk lives at the **application layer**, built on top of existing AI infrastructure — not competing with it.
